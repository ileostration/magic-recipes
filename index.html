Credits & terms

The Grimoire of Delicacies is a free, non-commercial project. It runs entirely in your browser: there are no accounts, and what you read, search, bookmark, write or draw is remembered only in this browser’s local storage so the book can open to your tastes next time.

Recipes

The grimoire’s own recipes are written for this project. Their ingredient lists are plain lists of facts, and their methods are written in our own words while keeping the real process, temperatures and timings. They are offered as general cooking guidance without warranty; check doneness and food safety yourself. Nutrition figures are estimates computed from the ingredient lists.

Recipes marked Wikibooks are fetched live from the Wikibooks Cookbook and are reused under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each such page links back to its source.

Photographs and pictures

Dish photographs come from Unsplash under the Unsplash License, with the photographer named under each picture. The meadow ground and the tarot cards are pictures supplied by the project owner.

Software

Meadow rendered with three.js (MIT licence). Nearest-neighbour table built with scikit-learn. Drawing search uses a Quick, Draw! doodle classifier run in the browser with transformers.js, fetched from a public CDN on first use. Type: Caesar Dressing, Afacad Flux and Noto Sans Phoenician via Google Fonts.
