Credits & terms

The Grimoire of Delicacies is a free, non-commercial project. It runs entirely in your browser: there are no accounts, and what you read, search, bookmark or write is remembered only in this browser’s local storage so the book can open to your tastes next time.

Recipes

The grimoire’s own recipes are written for this project. Their ingredient lists are plain lists of facts, and their methods are written in our own words while keeping the real process, temperatures and timings. They are offered as general cooking guidance without warranty; check doneness and food safety yourself. Nutrition figures are estimates computed from the ingredient lists and are not a substitute for a dietitian.

Recipes marked Wikibooks are fetched live from the Wikibooks Cookbook and are reused under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. Each such page links back to its source.

Photographs

Dish photographs come from Unsplash under the Unsplash License, with the photographer named under each picture. Live Wikibooks recipes show Wikimedia Commons photos with author and licence. The tarot cards are illustrations supplied by the project owner.
