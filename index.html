Credits & terms

The Recipe Book is a free, non-commercial project. It runs entirely in your browser: there are no accounts, and what you read or search is remembered only in this browser’s local storage so the book can open to your tastes next time. Clear your site data to forget it.

Recipes

The book’s own recipes are written for this project. Their ingredient lists are plain lists of facts, and their methods are written in our own words while keeping the real process, temperatures and timings. They are offered as general cooking guidance without warranty; check doneness and food-safety yourself.

Recipes marked Wikibooks are fetched live from the Wikibooks Cookbook and are reused under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. Each such page links back to its source. Any changes we make to that text (such as splitting it across pages) are shared under the same licence.

Photographs

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and are shown with the author and licence named under each picture, linking to the file page. No photograph is stored on our servers.
